The firmware images under `src/firmware` are unmodified binary releases from
Microchip's MPLAB Harmony `smartenergy` repository. The PL460 boot protocol,
mailbox protocol, and G3 PHY wire formats were adapted from Microchip Harmony
driver sources. These parts are covered by `LICENSE-MICROCHIP.md`; in
particular, they may be used only with or in combination with a Microchip
device. This library is intended to control the Microchip PL460.

The Arduino transport, portable C++ API, examples, and documentation were
created for this project. No claim is made that Microchip has tested or
endorsed this Arduino port.
